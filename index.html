1. Terms of Service

This agreement is between NucleusEd and the person who books. For students under 18, that's the parent or guardian.

Payment

Single sessions are paid when you book. Blocks and packages are paid upfront. If a payment fails, upcoming sessions are paused until it's settled.

Cancelling & rescheduling

Reschedule free with at least 24 hours' notice. Inside 24 hours or no-shows are charged in full. If I cancel, you choose a new time or a refund. Late arrivals still finish at the scheduled time.

Refunds & your 14-day right to cancel

You have 14 days after booking online to cancel. If lessons start sooner at your request, you pay only for sessions already delivered. Unused block sessions are refundable on request.

Price changes

I'll give at least 30 days' notice. Sessions you've already paid for keep their price.

Holidays & ending lessons

Regular slots are held over school holidays if you let me know. Either side can stop with one week's notice.

Results

I'll teach to the highest standard and track progress closely. Grades also depend on independent study, so specific results can't be guaranteed.

Teaching materials

Notes, worksheets and recordings are for the student's own use. Please don't share or resell them.

2. Online Lessons & Group Coaching

Online lessons

Lessons run on Zoom / Google Meet. Students should be in a quiet, shared space with a parent at home. If connection fails, time is made up accordingly.

Recording

Lessons are only recorded with a parent's written consent. Recordings are shared only with the family and deleted after 30 days.

Group conduct

Be respectful, keep cameras on, and never screenshot or record other students. I may remove a student who breaks these rules.

3. Safeguarding

Kayal holds an Enhanced DBS certificate. You can view or verify it before your first paid session.

How I communicate

By email, or a WhatsApp group with a parent included. I never message students privately on social media.

If I'm worried about a child

Any welfare concern is shared with parents and, where needed, the local authority's safeguarding team.

In-person lessons

A parent or carer should be at home during lessons, which take place in a shared space.

4. Privacy & UK GDPR

What I collect and why

Names, contact details, school year, exam board and progress notes, used only to deliver tutoring. Kept for 2 years after lessons end, then securely deleted.

Who else handles it

Stripe (payments), Cal.com (bookings) and email providers. Your data is never sold.

Reviews & photos

Testimonials are only published with written permission, using first names and initials.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing info@nucleused.co.uk.

5. Questions & Complaints

Have a question or concern?

Email info@nucleused.co.uk. I'll reply within 2 working days and aim to resolve it promptly.
